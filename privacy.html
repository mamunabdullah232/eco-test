YOUR LEARNING SPACE

Privacy, in plain language.

Last updated: 27 October 2026.

Public practice

The public website does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or a login to practise. Quiz answers and scores are calculated in your browser. The public quiz pages do not send your attempts to a Xohopathi account database.

External websites

YouTube, textbook libraries, question papers and official examination portals open as external links. Those services have their own privacy policies. YouTube videos are not embedded automatically on this website.

Private owner workspace

The owner workspace contains an encrypted archive. Its access key is used locally in the browser and is not sent to a server. Unlocked contents are held for the current session and cleared when the workspace is locked. The workspace is for its owner; it is separate from the public practice library.

Website hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process normal web-request information under its own privacy policy. Xohopathi does not add advertising trackers or an analytics service to these pages.

Independent educational content

Xohopathi is an independent learning platform. Practice questions are learning materials. Official exam notifications and the relevant examination authority determine eligibility, syllabus and selection requirements.
